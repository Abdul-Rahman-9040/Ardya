# Property Photo Assets

The uploaded Ardya property photos are optimized as WebP files in `public/images/property/`.
Their room assignments and gallery captions are maintained in `src/data/propertyPhotos.json`.

- `arabica-1/` and `arabica-2/` contain Arabica room photos.
- `robusta-premium-1/` and `robusta-premium-2/` contain Robusta Premium room photos.
- `surroundings/` contains exterior and estate photos.

Room pages, the home page room cards, About page, and filterable photo gallery use this
manifest. Keep the original uploads in `assets/`; do not move or rename the video files
when updating property photos.
